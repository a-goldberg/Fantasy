# Provisional 2026 keeper assessment

Status: Scenario analysis only.  No keeper has been declared, and the league-wide keeper list is not yet available.

- Justin Herbert in round 2 costs Goldberg's pick 17.
- Matthew Stafford in round 7 costs Goldberg's pick 64.

The sources disagree enough that a simple mean would be misleading.  Even so, they agree on the decision shape:

- Stafford at pick 64 is a large value relative to all four snapshots.  Unless new health or role information changes his outlook, he is the clear provisional keeper.
- Herbert at pick 17 is close to market price rather than an obvious bargain.  One source puts him earlier than the cost; three place him at or after it.

The first transparent base composite generated on Aug. 16 ranks Herbert 26th overall and Stafford 39th.  That base board measures player quality, not expected availability, and therefore should not be treated as a mock draft.  It reinforces the same provisional conclusion: Stafford at pick 64 is a material value, while Herbert at pick 17 requires keeper-driven scarcity to justify the cost.  Their bye weeks (7 and 11) are compatible.

## Draft-order effect

If Herbert is not kept, Goldberg has only a seven-pick gap between picks 17 and 24.  The managers making those six intervening selections are Greenspan, Tompkins, and Ori.  Ori has historically taken his first live QB in round 1.75; Greenspan in 2.88; Tompkins in 3.88.  One or more quarterbacks may disappear, but the historical pattern does not imply that an entire current QB tier will necessarily vanish in those seven picks.

That makes the working strategy:

1. Keep Stafford in the seventh, subject to preseason health and starting-role confirmation.
2. Leave Herbert undecided until the other keeper declarations are known.
3. If several top-12 QBs are kept, Herbert at 17 becomes more defensible because the live pool is compressed.
4. If few top QBs are kept, preserve pick 17 for the best available QB/RB/WR and plan to take a comparable QB at pick 24 if the board calls for it.

- Confirmation that Goldberg's 2026 picks have not been traded.
